How every idea is judged

  • Rules first: each test's rules and pass thresholds are pushed to the repo before any result is seen.
  • Against chance: zone tests compare with random levels, trade tests with random entries, and continuation with a random-walk rate.
  • After costs: trades fill at real bid and ask prices, with an assumed commission.
  • Enough evidence: zone and continuation tests need at least 300 to 1,100 events, and trade tests at least 300 trades, with a 95% range that excludes zero.
  • One holdout: November 2023 onward is untouched, for a single final check of anything that passes.
