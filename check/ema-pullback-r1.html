Draft rules

  1. 1-hour candles. Trend filter: at least 80 of the last 100 closes above the 200 SMA (blue line) for longs, below it for shorts.
  2. Structure: the last 4 swings are low A, high B, low C, high D, with D above B and C above A. Swings need 5 candles each side. Shorts mirror this.
  3. Swing size: each leg (A to B, B to C, C to D) is at least 1.5 ATR, so no swing is tiny.
  4. The 50 EMA zone is the 50 EMA (orange line) plus and minus 0.5 ATR (orange band). Low C was inside it, so the trend has already pulled back to the EMA once.
  5. After D, price dips into the zone, then a down candle is followed by an up candle closing at least half the down candle's body above its close.
  6. Entry at the next candle's open. Stop 1 ATR below the 50 EMA, target 1.5R.
  7. Cancelled by a close below the stop level, the trend filter failing, the next swing forming, or 100 candles without entry.

How to mark

  • Right: this is the setup you meant, found where you would have found it.
  • Wrong: it isn't. Say why in the note, for example which swing or candle is off.
  • Each chart stops where the setup is known. What happened next is hidden on purpose, so judge the setup, not whether it would have paid.
  • Marks save in this browser as you go. When you're done, Send marks opens a GitHub issue in the private repo with them filled in. Press Submit there.
