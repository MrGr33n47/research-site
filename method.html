Rules first

Each test's rules and pass marks are written and pushed to the private repo before any result is seen. A change after a result makes a new test, not a fix.

Against chance

Zone tests compare with random levels, trade tests with random entries in the same conditions, and continuation with a random walk from the same starting point.

After costs

Trades fill at real bid and ask prices on 1-minute candles, with an assumed 0.7 pip commission. A minute that hits both stop and target counts as a stop.

Enough evidence

A pass needs enough events (300 trades, or 1,100 zone touches), an edge clearly above zero on a month-block 95% range, and consistency across pairs and periods.

Your eye

Slideshows let you mark real setups blind. Marks shape the rules, which are then tested on data the marks never saw.

One holdout

November 2023 onward is untouched, for a single final check of anything that passes.

Reading the numbers

  • R: the amount risked on a trade. -1R is a full loss, and +1.5R a win at a 1.5R target.
  • Edge in points: how many percentage points more often something happened than chance would give.
  • 95% range: where the true value plausibly lies, given the sample. A range that includes zero means no edge is shown.
