Trend pullback zones

In a trend of 2 full swings with pullbacks to the 50 EMA, does the broken swing turn price back with the trend?

OverviewTestsRule checksMethodData

All tests · 2026-11-03 · 7 majors, 2010 to Oct 2023

Failed Failed, and worse than random without the EMA rule.

Rules tested

  1. The last 5 swings make higher highs and higher lows, or the mirror for downtrends.
  2. Both pullback lows reach the 1-hour 50 EMA, and price closes above a rising EMA.
  3. The zone is the high the newest high broke, plus 0.25 ATR each side.
  4. Touches from above are scored 1 ATR with the trend against 1 ATR against it.
