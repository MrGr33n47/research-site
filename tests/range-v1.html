Clear 1-hour ranges

Once a range has 2 touches on each side, do later touches turn back more often than random levels?

Failed Closed under this definition.

Rules tested

  1. 1-hour candles, swings of 5 candles each side.
  2. The last 4 swings alternate, giving 2 tops and 2 bottoms.
  3. The tops are within 1 ATR of each other, the bottoms likewise, and at least 2.5 ATR apart.
  4. Each zone is its swings plus 0.25 ATR, and the range ends at a close 0.5 ATR beyond a zone.
  5. Touches count from inside the range, numbered from 3, and are scored 1 ATR back against 1 ATR onward.
